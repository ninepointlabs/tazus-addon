The Shaman Bar

Imbue, Fire Nova, shield and totems. Four small cells, one press at a time.

Your weapon, your shield, your totems.

A small bar sits next to your action bars. Two cells are buttons you press; two are hints that tell you what's missing. It sits beside Blizzard's own totem tracker and the Call of the Elements bar, and never replaces them: you still drop totems from your bars.

The four cells

  1. Weapon imbue button. One press out of combat applies the recommended imbue at your highest rank. Following Blizzard's Nov 10/11 hotfix, Windfury Weapon is at the heart of Enhancement; Rockbiter Weapon is the leveling fallback before 30. The cell shows the time left.
  2. Fire Nova button. Lit only while one of your Fire totems is down. In Forever, Fire Nova Totem is removed: Fire Nova erupts at your Fire totem instead.
  3. Shield hint. Lightning Shield or Water Shield, how many orbs are left, and a refresh me when it's gone.
  4. Totems hint. Which Earth, Fire, Water and Air totem fits Solo, Dungeon or Raid, with a nudge when one is missing.

Imbues in Forever

  • Imbues last 60 minutes.
  • They now stack with weapon totems.
  • Prefer another imbue? Pin it under Settings › Shaman bar.

Blizzard's rules, kept

  • Advice only: one key press is one cast. Nothing casts on its own, there's no /click, and no macros are created.
  • The hints are hints, not buttons. They never cast anything.
  • Keys are optional and none is bound by default. Pick one under Settings › Shaman bar. Super (Meta) combos are never used.

      The Tavern

      Questions, answered by the innkeeper.

      The innkeeper

      Welcome, Shaman! Leave your totems by the door. What would you like to know?

      Does it work on Classic Era or retail?

      No. It's built for World of Warcraft: Forever only (Interface 16001), and other clients won't load it. Forever's Shaman differs from Classic: Fire Nova Totem is gone, and imbues now stack with weapon totems.

      Is this automation?

      No. One key press is one cast. The imbue and Fire Nova buttons only cast when you click them or press their key. The Shield and Totems hints, the rotation helper and the next-spell HUD only advise. No /click, and no macros are created.

      Does it replace Blizzard's totem tracker?

      No. Blizzard's built-in totem tracker and the Call of the Elements bar stay the way to see and drop your totems. The Totems hint only says which totem fits and nudges you when one is missing.

      Why Rockbiter Weapon and not Windfury?

      Before 30, Rockbiter Weapon is the leveling fallback. After Blizzard's Nov 10/11 hotfix, Windfury Weapon is at the heart of Enhancement. Want something else? Pin it under Settings › Shaman bar.

      Why is the Fire Nova button dark?

      Because no Fire totem is down. In Forever, Fire Nova erupts at your Fire totem, so the button only lights up while one is out.

      Why don't the buttons have keys?

      Keys are optional and none is bound by default, so it never steals a key you already use. Pick one under Settings › Shaman bar. Super (Meta) combos are never offered: on Omarchy and many Linux desktops they belong to the desktop.

      What does it cost?

      Nothing. It's free and open source under the MIT license.

      Is there one for other classes?

      Yes, from the same workshop: Andric's Addon for Priests, Parzelle's Addon for Mages and Tristan's Addon for Paladins. And My Azeroth Life is where the whole adventure lives.

      Is this made by Blizzard?

      No. Tazu's Addon is an unofficial, free and open source fan project by Nine Point Labs. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.
